Delaunay — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short. Delaunay is a game with no account, no ads and no purchases.

Your progress and settings stay on your device.

If, and only if, you agree when the app asks, it shares anonymous play statistics and crash reports to help improve the game. You can change your mind at any time in the app's Settings.

1. Who is responsible

Delaunay is published by Loïc Ciccone, an individual developer ("I", "me"). For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact.lcapps@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to me or to anyone else:

  • your puzzle progress and stars;
  • your local match statistics and your last match setup;
  • your settings (music and sound volume, vibration), which tutorials you have seen, and your answer to the statistics question.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

3. What is shared if you agree

On first launch the app asks whether you want to share statistics. Nothing described in this section is collected before you tap "Share", and nothing is collected if you tap "No thanks".

If you agree, the app sends the following to Google Firebase, a service of Google that I use to understand how the game is played and to fix problems:

WhatDetails
Play statistics
(Google Analytics for Firebase)		 Which puzzle levels are started, and how each attempt ends (cleared or stuck, score, stars). How local matches are set up (number of human players and bots, bot levels, board, walls) and how they end (winner type, top score, duration). Whether a tutorial was opened, skipped or completed. Totals of levels cleared, stars and matches played. General usage such as first open, sessions and time spent in the app.
Crash reports
(Firebase Crashlytics)		 When the app crashes or hits an error: the technical trace of the error, the time, and the state of the app and device at that moment.
Device and app information Device model, operating system version, language, app version, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. Google does not store the IP address itself in Analytics.
Identifiers A random identifier created for this installation of the app, so that events from the same installation can be counted together. It is not your name, phone number or email, and it is reset if you reinstall the app.

4. What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your precise location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Your advertising identifier. The app shows no ads, and advertising features of the analytics service are switched off.

I do not sell your data, and I do not share it with anyone other than the service provider named in section 6.

5. Why the data is used, and your choice

The statistics and crash reports are used only to understand how the game is played, find problems and improve the game. The legal basis is your consent.

You can withdraw it at any time: on the home screen, open Settings (the gear button) and switch off Share statistics. Collection stops immediately. The game works exactly the same whether you share or not.

6. Who processes the data

The data is processed on my behalf by Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere), as part of Firebase. Google may process it on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its own terms.

  • Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Google Privacy Policy

7. How long it is kept

  • Play statistics: Google Analytics keeps event-level data for a limited period (2 months by default, 14 months at most), after which only aggregated figures remain.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the data about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, write to the address in section 1. Because the data is tied only to a random installation identifier, I may need your help to find it.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Delaunay is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them. No data is collected without the choice described in section 3.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if new features are added, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
